# Vision and scope

## Product

DRVO ERP is a modular SaaS business operating system for multiple industries, businesses and branches.

## Product principles

- One tenant may operate multiple businesses of different types and multiple branches.
- Reusable core modules; industry-specific extensions for distinct workflows.
- Clear and fast UX, inspired by Odoo's navigation and reuse patterns, with DRVO's own design system.
- Ship end-to-end working slices quickly, without speculative abstractions.

## Initial verticals

1. **Salon:** POS, staff commissions, booking and queue workflows.
2. **Retail:** POS with barcode, catalog and inventory workflows.
3. **Clinic:** future discovery, distinct requirements and sensitive-data controls.

## Non-goals of the initial phase

- Microservices infrastructure
- A complete accounting suite before first sale flow
- Simultaneous launch for every industry
- Copying old Cashier architecture unchanged
