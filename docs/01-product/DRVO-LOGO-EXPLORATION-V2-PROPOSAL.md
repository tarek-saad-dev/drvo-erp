# DRVO ERP logo exploration V2 — three alternatives

**Status:** PROPOSED for founder comparison, 2026-11-10. Not adopted; ADR-024 palette unchanged.

## Three distinct directions

1. **Modular Core** — modular elements connected around shared center; symbol works independently for App Icon and as a master brand; suitable for multi-industry and flexible software architecture. Source: [SVG](../../assets/brand/explorations/drvo-v2-modular-core.svg).
2. **Distinctive D** — proprietary letterform direction with a Teal forward path, pairing strong D icon with DRVO ERP wordmark. Avoid presenting the arrow as a promise of results. Source: [SVG](../../assets/brand/explorations/drvo-v2-d-monogram.svg).
3. **Custom Wordmark** — typography-led identity using a Teal V instead of a separate standalone symbol; highly legible full name but needs a derived small-size icon. Source: [SVG](../../assets/brand/explorations/drvo-v2-custom-wordmark.svg).

## Palette and guardrails

All use ADR-024 Plum `#74536E`, Deep Plum `#553E53`, Teal `#1A7B80`, subtle neutral strokes. No salon-exclusive icon or claim of support for unverified industries. Preserve globally recognizable DRVO parent brand.

## Approval gate

- Founder chooses one direction for further development — this document does **not** supersede the prior proposed V1 logo or constitute an approved final brand.
- Complete SVG optical correction and text-to-outline using a licensed font, 16px favicon and avatar tests, light/dark monochrome variants, accessibility, trademark and similarity clearance.
- Only after approval sync ADR/design docs, current state and marketing assets; no changes to app theme or deployed product without separate authorization.

No code, SQL, tenant permissions, deployment or publication changes.
