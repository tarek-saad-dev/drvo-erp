## Needed follow-ups before product blueprint sign-off

**F1 — Full ERP scope:** Which capability areas must eventually be first-party (accounting/GL, payroll, inventory, purchasing, CRM, ecommerce, manufacturing, etc.) and which can remain integrations? Distinguish 1-year ambition from initial MVP.

**F2 — Vertical sequence:** After barber salons, is the intended second vertical aesthetic/beauty, medical clinics, or supermarkets? Clinic regulation/data sensitivity has implications.

**F3 — Stakeholder applications:** Are customer portals, employee portals, partner dashboards and owner apps part of initial MVP or later releases?

**F4 — Egypt + Gulf launch:** Simultaneous commercial launch or Egypt-first then particular Gulf country? Which Gulf countries first?

**F5 — Mobile experience:** Responsive web/PWA first, or native Android/iOS apps in first release?

**F6 — Modular contracts:** Does each industry require entirely independent extensions for every module, or do modules expose optional capability/policy extension points when genuinely needed?

**F7 — Success measurement:** Which concrete outcomes should improve at 3 months and at 12 months? Choose baselines and targets after discovery.

**F8 — MVP boundary:** What must a chain of barber salons be able to do before replacing or piloting alongside Cashier?

**F9 — Product differentiation:** Which Odoo workflows are non-negotiable in V1, and what value must DRVO add beyond them?

**F10 — Target complexity:** Typical number of branches, employees and checkout/booking transactions per business in year one?

These are open questions, not inferred decisions.
